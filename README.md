# Customer Prioritization & Revenue Strategy

## Business Problem
Companies often run retention campaigns for all customers equally.  
However, revenue is highly concentrated and uniform engagement wastes resources.

This project identifies which customers deserve attention and estimates the financial impact of targeted actions.

---

## Objective
Build a decision framework that helps the business:
- Identify high-value customers
- Prioritize retention vs upsell vs low effort
- Estimate potential revenue impact
- Produce operational targeting lists

---

## Methodology

### Step 1 — Customer Metrics
We created behavioral features:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary

### Step 2 — Revenue Concentration
Pareto analysis revealed revenue inequality.

### Step 3 — Segmentation
Customers mapped into action groups:

| Segment | Action |
|------|------|
| Immediate Retention | Prevent churn |
| Maintain Loyalty | Protect value |
| Upsell Opportunity | Grow revenue |
| Nurture | Light engagement |
| Minimal Effort | Low priority |

### Step 4 — Financial Impact
Estimated influence of targeted actions rather than predicting churn.

---

## Key Insight
A small portion of customers generates most revenue.  
Protecting loyal customers has higher impact than chasing churn.

---

## Deliverables
- Customer prioritization framework
- Operational targeting lists
- Impact estimation dashboard
